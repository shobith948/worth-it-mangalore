    About Worth It: Mangalore

    Mangalore (Kudla) is home to some of the richest coastal cuisine on Earth, yet 90% of online food guides are AI-scraped copies of Zomato reviews, tourist traps, or paid restaurant PR reels.

    Our Philosophy:

    • We Pay Our Own Bills: Every single meal price listed on this site is what came out of our pocket.
